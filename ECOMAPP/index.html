ECOMAPP (Citizen Application)

Designed to enhance citizens' preparedness and response during emergencies through self-assessment, structured incident reporting, and off-grid mesh communication.

Self-Assessment

Short guided questionnaire evaluating location, health status, and surroundings to provide tailored safety steps.

M/ETHANE Reporting

Send structured incident reports with hazard details, photos, and exact GPS coordinates directly to first responders.

Nearby Chat (Mesh)

Peer-to-peer Bluetooth/Wi-Fi mesh networking allowing direct messages even when cellular networks fail.

First Aid Guides

Categorized step-by-step guidance (Critical, Urgent, Minor) for CPR, severe bleeding, choking, and burns.

Field Operations

ECOMAPP-FR (First Responders)

Operational counterpart designed for rescue teams, providing real-time situational picture, on-scene triage, and route navigation.

Live Incident Map

Real-time display of citizen reports as color-coded pins. Filters by time/status with built-in navigation to the location.

START Protocol Triage

JOAFG Co-design

Co-designed with Johanniter Austria (JOAFG). Rapid casualty categorization via QR scanning & anatomical body diagram.

Traffic Re-routing

RoadTwin Integration

Integrated with RoadTwin systems to display real-time alternative routes and road closures during crisis transport.
